%% NORMAL 出边

NORMAL -->|r>100 / heavyCount++, overCount++| OVER

NORMAL -->|-100≤r≤-80 / reverseHeavyCount++| R_HEAVY

NORMAL -->|r<-100 / reverseHeavyCount++, reverseOverCount++| R_OVER

%% =====================

HEAVY -->|-100≤r≤-80 / reverseHeavyCount++| R_HEAVY

HEAVY -->|r<-100 / reverseHeavyCount++, reverseOverCount++| R_OVER



%% =====================
%% OVER_LOAD 出边
